Infrastructure Comparison

Clinical Payments & Alternative Assets for Fertility Care

BTC Supported (Square)

Bitcoin (BTC) Entry

Square: Native support via Lightning Network (BitPay/CashApp). Settlements can be converted to USD instantly.

NexHealth: No native BTC support in clinical links. Limited to traditional fiat.

EMR Integration

Square: Zero native sync. Requires manual ledger entries for every $15k cycle.

NexHealth: 1-to-1 sync with clinical software. Automated patient reconciliation.

Financial Verdict

Square wins on Payment Versatility. NexHealth wins on Operational Efficiency.

Strategic Capability Mapping

While Square offers broader consumer tech features like **Bitcoin acceptance** and retail hardware, NexHealth is a specialized medical tool. For a fertility clinic, the tradeoff is between "accepting everything" (Square) and "automating everything" (NexHealth).

BTC Note:

Accepting BTC on Square typically incurs a 1% processing fee if using Lightning partners, often cheaper than standard 2.6% credit card swipes.
